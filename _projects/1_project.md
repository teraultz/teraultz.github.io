## Overview

Lost Memories is a first-person escape room game developed in Unreal Engine that introduces players to the concepts and importance of two factor authorization (2FA) through interactive puzzles and exploration. Players begin the game having lost their memories of their identity and must work their way through an unfamilair environment to uncover what happened. To progess through the escape room, players must answer a series of identity based questions presented on panels beside the escape rooms locked doors. Finding the answers requires the player to explore their surroundings, investigate all the interactable objects, and collect information about themselves that helps them piece togetehr their forgotten identity. As players progress through the game, they gradually uncover more about the mystery behind their situation.

The games educational focus is incorporated directly into its progression system. Players must demonstrate their undertsanding of the information they have gathered to unlock new areas, while each door panel also provides additional information about 2FA and its role in protecting personal accounts. This project was designed with high school and college students as its target audience, using an escape room format to make cybersecurity education more interactive and engaging.

## Core Gameplay Concept

Players progress through the escape room by solving identity based puzzles:

1. Review the Questions: Examine the 2FA questions displayed on the panels beside eached locked door.
2. Explore the Envrionment: Investigate the room and interact with highlighted objects to uncover information.
3. Review Collected Information: Read through the memories of the discovered objects in the players notebook.
4. Solve the Puzzles: Piece together the memories gathered throughout the room to figure out the answers to the 2FA questions.
5. Unlock the Ntex Area: Enter the correct answers into the 2FA panel to unlock the door and continue exploring.

## My Contributions

I was responsible for all of the games programming and contributed to the design and implementation of its gameplay experience:

- Gameplay Programming: Implemented the games core mechanics and gameplay systems in Unreal Engine using blueprints.
- Puzzle Design: Collaborated on designing puzzles centered around 2FA questions.
- Environment Development: Built and assembled the games environments using a mix of free 3D assets and assets developed by a team member.
- Narrative Integration: Integrated the games narrative progression based on storyline developed by a team member.
- Interactive Object System: Programmed the interactive objects and implemented the functionality that allows discovered items to appear in the players notebook.
- Playtesting &amp; Iteration: Conducted playtesting and refined the player experience based on observations and feedback.
- Debugging &amp; Problem Solving: Identified, investigated, and resolved technical issues throughout development. 

## Retrospection

One of the biggest challenges during development was getting the interactive objects that the player could click n to appear in the players notebook. This required figuring out how to connect the players interactions with the games information collection system, ensuring that objects could be examined and access their details in the notebook. This project was the first ever game I developed. Through this experience, I gained my first understanding of everything that goes into developing a game, from programming the mechanics and systems to designing the gameplay experiences bring the game together.

My proudest accomplishment from this project was simply completing my first game. Although there were many areas that could have been improved, successfully taking this project from its initial concept to a finished, playable game showed me what I was capable of accomplishing as a developer. It gave me the confidence to pursue game development further and helped establish the foundation of what makes me a game developer and designer.
