## Overview

Paper Toss VR is a first-person VR game designed to bring the player into the world of the app game Paper Toss. This game was made in Unity. The purpose of this game was to combine everything we learned in class so we could make a fun VR experience for the player. The game starts the player off in the tutorial space where they learn the controls and how to beat a level by making several shots into the office trash bin. The second level then ups the difficulty by adding in the fan obstacle, which rotates and blows the paper balls being tossed away from the trash bin. Then the third level adds in office workers who walk back and forth, blocking the player's shot. Along with the trash bin starting to teleport between 3 spots, requiring the player to utilize VR's spatial features when trying to make the shot.

This game was a university project, centered around getting its students familiar with VR in Unity. We learned new things each week and then started this project halfway into the semester.

## Core Gameplay Concept

The player proceeds through a level by:

- Grabbing a paper ball from the spawn point
- Throwing the paper ball to make the shot while avoiding obstacles
- Pressing a button to spawn more paper balls
- Repeat the process until the player makes the required number of shots to move on to the next level
